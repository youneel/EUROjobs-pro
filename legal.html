Privacy

EuroJobs Pro is an independent demo project. It does not have accounts, a database, or a server-side profile. The CV details you type, the cover letters you generate and the roles you save stay in your browser's local storage on this device.

What stays on your device

  • Your profile fields: name, headline, contact details, location, experience, skills, languages and summary.
  • Your saved roles and any cover-letter context passed through the page URL.
  • Draft text you type into the cover-letter editor. It is held in the page until you leave or refresh.

What leaves the browser

Nothing is sent to us. There is no analytics, advertising or remote AI service behind the toolkit. The page does request two public resources from third-party content networks:

  • Google Fonts, for the optional Inter typeface.
  • pdf.js from cdnjs, only if you choose a PDF CV; the file is read in the browser, not uploaded to us.

Those networks can see your IP address and browser request, as any website request would. If you prefer to avoid them, use the system font fallback and a TXT or Markdown CV. A production deployment should self-host these assets.

Clearing your data

Use Clear in CV Studio, or clear site data in your browser settings. Saved roles can be removed one at a time from the jobs page. External employment portals linked from this site have their own privacy policies.

This demo has no operator contact or data controller details. Add a monitored contact and a lawful basis before publishing it as a real service.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies of its own. It uses browser local storage, which is not a cookie and is not sent with every request.

Keys used

  • ejp_saved_v1 — the roles you save, so the heart count survives a refresh.
  • ejp_profile_v1 — the CV profile you choose to save on this device.

Both keys are readable only by pages on the same origin. You can delete them through your browser's site-data controls at any time. The cover-letter editor and advisor conversation are not persisted unless you copy or download the result.

Third-party requests

Google Fonts and the optional pdf.js download are ordinary HTTP requests. They may receive technical request data under their own policies. No tracking pixels or advertising scripts are included in this demo.

If you add analytics, embeds, or a real authentication service later, update this page and add a consent mechanism where the law requires one.

Accessibility

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA and to work with a keyboard, a screen reader, browser zoom and reduced-motion preferences.

What is in place

  • A skip link, visible focus styles, labelled form fields and landmark navigation.
  • Proper tab semantics for the toolkit, with arrow-key navigation and panel state announced.
  • Dialog semantics and focus return for the job-detail drawer; Escape closes menus and dialogs.
  • Reduced-motion support that disables decorative animation and smooth scrolling.
  • Live regions for search results, status messages and the visa advisor conversation.
  • Print styles that leave only the cover letter on the page.

Known limitations

  • Country flags are emoji, so their appearance depends on your operating system and font.
  • PDF CV reading depends on a third-party script; TXT and Markdown work without it.
  • Linked public employment portals are outside this project and may have their own accessibility gaps.
  • Very long generated letters may need manual trimming before printing or downloading.

This demo does not yet provide a monitored accessibility feedback channel. Add one before publishing it as a public service.
